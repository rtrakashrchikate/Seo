## Part 4: Weekly X / Twitter Content Calendar (First 30 Days)

Post 3–4 times per week. Use these themes:

| Week | Theme | Sample Tweet |
|------|-------|-------------|
| Week 1 | BNSS 2023 education | *"What changed in anticipatory bail under BNSS 2023? The biggest practical shift: 7-day notice to state is now mandatory at High Court level. This means you MUST file before arrest becomes imminent. Thread 🧵 #BNSS2023 #AnticipatoryBail #CriminalLaw"* |
| Week 1 | Pune statistics | *"Maharashtra registered 9,922 cyber FIRs in 2024 — second highest in India. Pune alone saw ₹6,007 crore in estimated losses. Cyber crime is not a minor matter. IT Act sections 66, 66C, 66D can mean non-bailable offences. #CyberLaw #Pune #Maharashtra"* |
| Week 2 | NDPS education | *"Section 37 NDPS twin test: to get bail in commercial quantity cases, court must find (1) reasonable grounds you're not guilty AND (2) you won't reoffend. BOTH conditions must be met. That's why NDPS commercial bail is so difficult. #NDPS #BailLaw #CriminalLaw"* |
| Week 2 | FIR quashing | *"Not every criminal FIR deserves to go to trial. Section 528 BNSS (formerly Section 482 CrPC) lets the Bombay HC quash FIRs that are: mala fide, civil disputes in criminal clothing, or without prima facie offence. The Bhajan Lal test still applies. #FIRQuashing #BNSS2023"* |
| Week 3 | BPSS 2023 | *"BPSS 2023 replaces the Indian Evidence Act. For cyber crime defendants, the key change: Section 63 electronic records. Prosecution must still certify digital evidence. Challenge the certification. Understand the chain of custody. #BPSS2023 #CyberLaw #DigitalEvidence"* |
| Week 3 | Practical tip | *"If you receive a notice from police or suspect an FIR against you: (1) Don't make statements without counsel present (2) Preserve all documentary evidence — contracts, messages, bank records (3) Contact a criminal lawyer BEFORE arrest if possible. #LegalTips #CriminalLaw #Pune"* |
| Week 4 | Cheque bounce | *"Cheque bounce under Section 138 NI Act is criminal, not civil. Yes, there are defences — no legally enforceable debt, cheque given as security, defective legal notice. But once a complaint is filed, it's in a magistrate court. Defence matters from day one. #ChequeBounce #NIAct"* |
| Week 4 | Bombay HC | *"Filed 27+ matters at Bombay High Court including bail applications, anticipatory bail hearings, and FIR quashing petitions. The HC has its own rhythms — timing a filing, crafting the grounds, and knowing which bench to expect matters enormously in practice. #BombayHighCourt"* |
